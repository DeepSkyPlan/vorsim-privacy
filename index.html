Privacy Policy — VOR Sim

Last updated: 1 August 2026

VOR Sim is a VOR and NDB radio navigation trainer. It is designed to work without collecting your personal data.

Data we collect

None. VOR Sim does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data that stays on your device

Everything the app remembers stays on your device, in its private storage: the home airfield you choose (its code and position), your instrument and view settings, and the state of the current flight. None of it is transmitted off your device.

Network use

The airport and navaid database is bundled with the app and works entirely offline. The only network requests VOR Sim makes are the map tiles and place names drawn by Apple’s MapKit on the moving map. Those requests go to Apple and are covered by Apple’s privacy policy; they carry the map area being displayed, which is the simulated training area, not your position unless you have chosen to centre it there.

Permissions

Location (When In Use) — optional, and used once: to centre the training area on where you are, so the exercise stations are places you recognise. The position is kept on your device and never sent anywhere. Decline it and the app centres on EDAZ Schönhagen instead and works exactly the same.

VOR Sim asks for no other permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone, your photo library, your contacts, or notifications.

Sound

The engine sound is generated by the app itself. It plays in the ambient audio category, which means it mixes with whatever else you are playing and respects the silent switch. Nothing is recorded.

Not for navigation

VOR Sim is a training aid. The airspace and the exercise stations are invented for training, and nothing in the app may be used for real navigation.

Children

VOR Sim does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes
